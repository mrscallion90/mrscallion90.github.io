About

About this project

A student prototype built to make money basics easier to find, read and use.

Purpose

Why it exists

Duit Sense started as a class project for CRM1953 Human-Computer Interaction at the German-Malaysian Institute. The brief was a financial literacy awareness website. The audience was easier to define than the content: Malaysians aged 18 to 30 who are studying, starting their first job, or doing both.

Most money advice assumes you already have money, a salary, or a parent who explained it. This site assumes none of that. It explains the five ideas that come up first, then gives you one thing to do with them.

Everything here is written in plain English, uses ringgit, and avoids selling you a product.

Project details

Disclaimer

What this site is not

Educational prototype, not financial advice

Duit Sense is a student project. It does not provide personal financial, legal or tax advice, and it cannot tell you what to do with your money. Figures on the Budget page are examples, not recommendations. For decisions about your own situation, speak to a qualified adviser or an official service listed on the Resources page.
