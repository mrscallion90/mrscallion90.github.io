Scams & debt

Slow down before you send money

Most scams rely on speed and trust, not technical skill. If a message pushes you to act now, that is your cue to stop.

One rule covers most of it

Never share your TAC, PIN or password. Treat any request for them as a red flag, whoever appears to be asking.

Red flags

What it looks like

One flag is a reason to check. Two or more in the same message is a reason to stop.

Common patterns, not a complete list. New versions appear every month.
What you see Why it is a red flag
Act now or lose it Urgency is used to stop you from checking. A real offer survives a phone call.
Read me the TAC we just sent That code authorises a transaction. Whoever asks for it is asking for control of your account.
Guaranteed returns Investments carry risk. A guarantee is a sales line, not a fact.
Pay a small fee first Money that is already yours should not need a payment to release it.
Join our investment group on WhatsApp or Telegram Groups are easy to stage: fake profits, fake members, real pressure.
Log in through this link A link can copy the real page. Open the official app instead.
Your account is frozen. Stay on the line Fear keeps you on the call while someone moves your money.

Pressure is the tell

An organisation with nothing to hide can wait while you check. A scam cannot afford the delay.

If you already sent money

Call your bank immediately. The sooner it is reported, the more chance there is of stopping the transfer.

What to do

Four steps, in order

  1. Stop the conversation

    Do not send more money and do not share more details. You do not owe anyone an explanation.

  2. Check through a channel you trust

    Use the official app, the number on the back of your card, or the organisation's own website. Never the link in the message.

  3. Report it

    Reporting helps even when no money was lost, because it can warn other people.

  4. Tell your bank and someone you trust

    Speed matters more than embarrassment. These operations are built to make you feel like you should stay quiet.

Debt

Debt, without the panic

Borrowing is not a character flaw. It is a tool with a price, and the price is easier to manage when you can see it.

Interest is the price of time. A small monthly instalment can hide a long and expensive repayment period, so ask for the total cost, not just the amount per month.

Minimum payments on a credit card are designed to keep the account open, not to clear it quickly.

A useful measure is the share of your income that goes to debt payments, known as the debt service ratio.

Talk to the bank early

If a payment is about to be missed, contact the lender before the due date. Waiting usually costs more. Official help is listed on the Resources page.
