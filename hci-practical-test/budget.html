Budget & save

Give every ringgit a job

A budget is a plan you make before the month starts. Here is one way to build it, with a sample you can adapt.

The split

Start with 50/30/20

A common guideline for dividing your take-home pay. Treat it as a starting point, not a rule.

50%

Needs

Rent, groceries, transport, utilities, phone, medicine. The costs that keep your life running.

30%

Wants

Eating out, subscriptions, leisure, clothes, gifts. The costs that make life better, not possible.

20%

Savings

Emergency fund, a savings goal, and paying down debt faster. Money you keep instead of spend.

If your needs take more than half, lower the other two until the plan balances. A budget you keep beats a perfect split you ignore.

Nothing left over is the goal

A balanced plan assigns every ringgit, including savings. It does not mean you spent everything.

Leave room for a bad week

A plan with no slack breaks the first time something goes wrong. Keep a small amount unassigned, or put it in the emergency fund.
