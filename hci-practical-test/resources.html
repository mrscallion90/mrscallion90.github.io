Resources

Where to check

Official Malaysian sources, a short glossary, and a few places to read more. All links go to the organisations' own sites.

Official

Start with these

Government and regulator sites. Free to use, no sign-up.

Financial Education Network

The Malaysian network behind the national financial literacy strategy. The main source for this site.

fenetwork.my

Bank Negara Malaysia

The central bank. Consumer information, and warnings about unlicensed financial services.

bnm.gov.my

AKPK

Credit counselling and debt management services for Malaysians who need help with repayments.

akpk.org.my

Glossary

Terms you will hear

Short definitions, in the order you are likely to meet them.

Plain-language definitions for this site. Official definitions may differ in detail.
Term What it means
BudgetA plan for your money, made before you spend it.
NeedsCosts you must cover to live and work: rent, food, transport, medicine.
WantsCosts that improve life but can wait: eating out, subscriptions, new clothes.
Emergency fundMoney set aside for unexpected essential costs, kept separate from daily spending.
InterestThe cost of borrowing money, usually charged as a percentage over time.
Compound interestInterest that is calculated on your interest as well as your original amount.
PrincipalThe original amount borrowed or saved, before any interest is added.
Debt service ratioThe share of your monthly income that goes to debt payments.
Minimum paymentThe smallest amount a lender accepts each month. The rest carries over and keeps costing.
CCRISBank Negara Malaysia's record of your loans and repayment history.
PhishingA fake message or website that tries to capture your login or card details.
TACA one-time code sent to confirm a transaction. It should never be shared.
